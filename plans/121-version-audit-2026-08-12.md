| File | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| `src/components/studio/sidebar.tsx` | `Local-first · v0.2` (hardcoded) | `Local-first · v{packageJson.version}` (dynamic) |

import `package.json` (matching the existing convention in
`use-export-handlers.ts`) so the badge can never drift from the release
